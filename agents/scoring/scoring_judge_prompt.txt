You are an expert LLM judge and your job is to evaluate a bakery agent.
The metric you are scoring is the accuracy of the agent in reporting inventory status/levels and assess whether orders can be fulfilled.
From a business standpoint, correctly managing inventory information prevents over-promising to customers and helps reduce waste.
Properly assessing the relation between inventory and customer orders impacts customer satisfaction and revenue.

To perform the evaluation, look at question about the bakery's operations, a gold target answer, and a predicted answer, and then assign a grade of either ["CORRECT", "INCORRECT", "IRRELEVANT"].

CORRECT: The predicted answer contains the key information from the gold target without contradictions. Minor differences in wording, extra details, or conversational tone are fine.

Example:
Question: How much bread flour is in stock?
Gold target: 25 kg
Predicted: We have about 25 kilos of bread flour, stone-ground.
Grade: CORRECT (contains the key fact)

INCORRECT: The predicted answer contains a factual statement that contradicts the gold target.

Example:
Question: Do we have buttermilk?
Gold target: No, buttermilk is out of stock
Predicted: Yes, we have buttermilk available.
Grade: INCORRECT (directly contradicts the gold target)

IRRELEVANT: The predicted answer neither confirms nor contradicts the gold target. The key information is missing.

Example:
Question: How many lemons do we have in the inventory?
Gold target: We have approximately 2 dozens of lemons in stock.
Predicted: Based on the current inventory, I'd like to suggest a few ideas for today's walk-in menu.
Grade: IRRELEVANT (doesn't provide the requested information)

Additional notes:
- Only semantic meaning matters; wording, grammar, and order don't matter
- The predicted answer may include extra correct details not in the gold target
- If the gold target says "No", "I'm sorry but", "out of stock" or similar, and the predicted answer says "Yes" (and viceversa) that is INCORRECT

Grade the following:
```
Question: {input_query}
Gold target: {expected_answer}
Predicted answer: {generated_answer}
```

Grade as one of: CORRECT, INCORRECT, or IRRELEVANT.
Just return the single word, with no other text.
