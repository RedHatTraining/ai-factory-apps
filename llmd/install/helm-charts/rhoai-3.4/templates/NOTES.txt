Next steps:

1. Wait for all operators to reach "Succeeded":

   watch "oc get csv -A --no-headers | \
     grep -E 'nfd|serverless|authorino|pipeline|rhods|custom-metrics|servicemesh' | \
     awk '{printf \"%-55s %s\n\", \$2, \$NF}'"

2. Verify the DSCI was auto-created by the RHOAI operator:

   oc get dsci

3. Apply the DataScienceCluster:

   oc apply -f helm-charts/rhoai-3.4/post-install/dsc.yaml

4. Wait for DSC to be ready (3-5 minutes):

   oc wait datasciencecluster default-dsc \
     --for=jsonpath='{.status.phase}'=Ready --timeout=300s

5. Verify RHOAI controllers are running:

   oc get pods -n redhat-ods-applications \
     -l 'app.kubernetes.io/name in (kserve-controller-manager,llmisvc-controller-manager,workload-variant-autoscaler)'

6. Run full validation:

   bash scripts/check-env.sh
